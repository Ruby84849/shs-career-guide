ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About SHS Career Guide

A web-based information guide designed to help Grade 10 students explore Senior High School educational pathways and possible careers.

🎓

Our Purpose

This website aims to make SHS track and career information easier to explore. It provides descriptions of learning pathways, an interest assessment, and examples of possible careers.

Who Can Use This Website?

Grade 10 students who want to learn more about their SHS options can use this guide as a starting point when planning their next academic step.

Important Reminder

This website is an informational guide. It does not replace official school enrollment information, professional career counseling, or advice from teachers and parents or guardians. Always verify the programs offered by your preferred school.
